# MVP Product Specification

## Goal

Validate whether two people in a long-distance relationship will repeatedly use a dedicated shared space because it makes everyday life feel more connected.

## Core flows

### 1. Pair
- Create account.
- Create a private pair invite.
- Partner accepts.
- Both confirm the relationship connection.
- Pair becomes active.

### 2. Home
The home screen should answer five questions at a glance:
- What time is it for my partner?
- How are they feeling?
- Are they available?
- What is our next planned moment?
- What changed in our shared world since I last opened the app?

### 3. Daily presence
Users can voluntarily share lightweight status such as mood, availability, local-time context and a short moment/photo.

### 4. Shared moment
Either partner can invite the other into a realtime voice, video or screen-sharing session. The receiving partner explicitly accepts before the session starts.

### 5. Shared life
Users can save photos, voice notes, plans, dates, places and memories into a private timeline visible only to the pair.

### 6. Private time
Either partner can pause optional presence sharing without penalty or explanation. The product must treat this as normal behaviour.

### 7. Disconnect
Either partner can disconnect the pair. Relationship-scoped sessions and sharing grants end immediately. Each user keeps access to their own account and exportable personal data.

## MVP screens

1. Welcome / value proposition
2. Sign up / sign in
3. Create pair / accept invite
4. Pair confirmation
5. Home / presence dashboard
6. Share a moment
7. Shared timeline
8. Plans / next meeting
9. Start shared session
10. Incoming shared-session request
11. Privacy & sharing controls
12. Account / data export / disconnect

## Not MVP

- Public profiles or follower graph
- Dating or matchmaking
- Expert relationship therapy content
- Large group rooms
- Public feed
- Gifts or virtual currency
- AI relationship scoring
- Complex gamification
- Advertising

## Product acceptance tests

The MVP is successful only if:
- a new user understands the product in under 30 seconds,
- pairing takes under 2 minutes,
- every realtime shared session clearly requires acceptance,
- optional sharing can be paused in one obvious action,
- the product remains useful when optional sharing is off,
- disconnect reliably ends pair-scoped access,
- and early couples return because they miss the shared space, not because notifications pressure them.
